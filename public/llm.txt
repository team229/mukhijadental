# Mukhija Dental and ENT centre

> Mukhija Dental and ENT centre is a multispecialty dental clinic in Model Town, Sonepat, Haryana, India. The clinic provides family dental care, including preventive, restorative, cosmetic, orthodontic, periodontal, endodontic and oral-surgery services. The canonical website is https://mukhijadentalclinic.com.

Phone: +91-7206722618
Email: info@mukhijadentalclinic.com
Location: Model Town, Sonepat, Haryana, India
Hours: Monday–Saturday: 9:00 AM–2:00 PM and 4:30–7:30 PM; Sunday: 9:00 AM–2:00 PM
Google rating shown on the website: 4.8/5

## Clinical team

- Dr. Bhupesh Mukhija — B.D.S., M.D.S.; Endodontist. The site identifies his clinical interests as root canal treatment, crowns, bridges and full-mouth rehabilitation.
