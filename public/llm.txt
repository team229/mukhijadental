# Mukhija Dental and ENT Centre (Mukhija Dental Clinic)

> Mukhija Dental and ENT Centre is a multispecialty dental clinic in Model Town, Sonepat, Haryana, India, led by Dr. Bhupesh Mukhija (Endodontist) and Dr. Shikha Mukhija (Periodontist & Implantologist). The clinic offers comprehensive family dental care — implants, cosmetic dentistry, root canal treatment, orthodontics, gum treatment, kids dentistry, maxillofacial surgery, and more — under one roof, serving patients across Sonipat, Murthal, Kundli, and surrounding sectors.
